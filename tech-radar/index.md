## Review method

Each report separates:

1. **Thoughtworks position** — the published blip, ring and rationale.
2. **Quality engineering interpretation** — implications for testing, delivery controls and QE operating models.
3. **Recommended action** — a personal Adopt, Trial, Assess or Caution position for further study.

These files are study notes, not official Thoughtworks publications.
