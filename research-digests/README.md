# Quality Engineering Research Digests

This directory contains dated research digests covering recent quality engineering articles, papers, technical reports and emerging industry practices.

## Naming convention

Files use the ISO date format:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD.md
```

The date represents when the digest was compiled. This convention keeps entries chronologically sortable and works well for future scheduled updates.

## Curation criteria

Each digest should:

1. Prefer reputable engineering organizations, primary research or established technical publications.
2. Focus on substantive developments relevant to quality engineering.
3. Distinguish source claims from independent interpretation.
4. Explain practical implications for QE strategy, tooling and operating models.
5. Preserve direct links to original sources.

## Suggested report structure

- Executive summary
- Selected articles or papers
- Quality engineering implications
- Trend synthesis
- Recommended reading order
- Practical actions for further study
- Source note
